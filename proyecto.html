About the project

Detecting earlier
to support healthier ageing

ACT-Early investigates the prevalence of sarcopenia and frailty, together with their individual markers and associated risk factors, among Diego Portales University workers aged 35 to 59.

The study seeks to generate evidence that may contribute to earlier identification and prevention before older age.
