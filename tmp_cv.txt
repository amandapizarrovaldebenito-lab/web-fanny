Providencia Mobile: +56 98431 1332; email: fanny.petermann@udp.cl                                                                                                 Web profile: ResearchGate, Google Scholar, ORCID   Fanny is a Chilean-Swiss researcher and dietitian with a wide international experience and background in public health, ageing, and lifestyle topics throughout the life cycle. She has had the opportunity to collaborate and study at different universities in South America and Europe (the University of Concepción, Chile and the University of Glasgow, United Kingdom). Fanny also speaks three different languages: Spanish (mother tongue), Italian (fluent) and English (fluent) and has deep knowledge in statistics (e.g., causal inference concepts, linear and logistics regression analyses, Cox proportional analyses, Poisson regression analyses, etc.), software programmes (advanced use of Stata, Endnote, GraphPad and an intermediate level of R) and other training (further knowledge in systematic review and meta-analysis). She has contributed as the first author or joint author in more than 316 scientific manuscripts (total citations= 34,045 h-index=64). Additionally, as she recognised that leadership is essential for tomorrow's future public health leaders, since 2016, she has been the deputy director of the international research consortium ELHOC (Epidemiology of Lifestyle and Health Outcomes in Chile). Since 2022, she has been a senior collaborator in the Global Burden of Disease Study. Both in 2024 and 2025, Fanny was recognised as part of the top 2% of the most-cited scientists worldwide in the ranking generated by Stanford University and published by Elsevier. In 2025 Fanny was also recognised as one of the top 100 Chileans in the AD Scientific Index (country rank: 83). Fanny also certified her leadership skills in the “Oxford Women’s Leadership Development Programme” in 2020.
