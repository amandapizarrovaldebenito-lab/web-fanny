Public Health & Epidemiology

Fanny Petermann-Rocha is a Chilean-Swiss public health researcher and dietitian with international experience in epidemiology, ageing and lifestyle across the life course. She is an Associate Professor at Diego Portales University and an Affiliate at the University of Glasgow. Her research focuses on modifiable lifestyle factors, chronic disease, sarcopenia and frailty, combining population-based epidemiology, biostatistics and evidence synthesis. She is Deputy Director of ELHOC and a Senior Collaborator of the Global Burden of Disease Study.
